About us

Built to make events easier, smarter, and more memorable.

Event Compass was created to simplify the complexity behind planning events by connecting people with trusted providers, clear communication, and better booking workflows.

Our mission

Bringing structure to the event planning experience.

Many event projects fail because the process is fragmented—vendors are hard to find, schedules are disorganized, and communication becomes chaotic. Event Compass brings everything into one platform so planners can move faster and service providers can deliver with confidence.

From intimate ceremonies to large corporate functions, our purpose is to help people create remarkable experiences without the operational stress.

What we solve

  • Slow discovery of dependable services
  • Scattered communication across different channels
  • Unclear booking and payment steps
  • Limited visibility for planning and reporting
